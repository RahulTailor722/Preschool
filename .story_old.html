Our story

Fourteen years, measured on the wall

Gulmohar began in 2011 in a rented two-room house off Bopal–Ambli Road — nine children and one very determined teacher. We have pencilled the doorframe every few years since.

  1. 2011

    9 children

    Two rented rooms

    One shelf of materials, one teacher, and a landlord who agreed to the noise.

  2. 2014

    42 children

    A garden of our own

    We moved, and the children planted the gulmohar we are named after.

  3. 2017

    128 children

    Three neighbourhoods

    Two more centres opened — on exactly the same ratios as the first.

  4. 2021

    214 children

    Back through the gate

    After eighteen months of screens, every classroom filled again.

  5. 2025

    340 children

    Six centres, same shelf

    Everything about us grew except the size of a class.

…and below the line, three numbers that never moved.
