## Photographs

All classroom photography is a single Indian Montessori shoot (Pexels IDs in the
`318644xx` range) — genuine Indian children in a genuine Montessori preschool, in
uniform and working with real materials. Using one shoot keeps lighting, uniforms and
colour consistent across the whole page. The five polaroid snapshots add younger Indian
children from other shoots, which is where a toddler face reads best.

> site advertises. This is a hard limit of free stock, not an oversight: Indian toddlers
> in a modern private play school essentially do not exist under a free licence. Searches

> preschool. For a real client, a half-day shoot at their own centre replaces all of it.

> ### ⚠️ Read this before publishing for a real client
>
> The people in the **staff** and **parent testimonial** photographs are stock models. The
> roles, quotes, ratings and credentials attached to them are invented demo content. (The
> site itself no longer names any individual.)
>
> Replace them with real photographs and real, permissioned quotes before going live for an
> actual school. Attaching an invented testimonial to a stock photograph of a real person
> misleads parents, and in India would also fall foul of the ASCI code and the Consumer
> Protection Act 2019 provisions on misleading endorsements.
>
> The same applies to the **"4.8★ from 312 parents"** rating and the `aggregateRating` block
> in the JSON-LD: publishing a fabricated review score as structured data is a Google Search
> policy violation and can get the site's rich results demoted or removed.

> Baloo 2 ships **no italic**. Nothing in the stylesheet asks for one; emphasis is done with
> colour and the `.swash` highlight instead. Adding `font-style: italic` to a display heading
> will get you a synthesised oblique, which looks broken on a rounded face.

| Library | Use | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [scroll-world](https://github.com/oso95/scroll-world) | Scroll-scrubbed banner engine | MIT (© 2026 cyw) |
| [GSAP 3.12.5](https://gsap.com/) + ScrollTrigger | Parallax and scrub-linked motion | GSAP Standard "No Charge" License |
| [Lenis 1.1.13](https://lenis.darkroom.engineering/) | Smooth scrolling | MIT |

GSAP and Lenis load from a CDN and are strictly optional — the site is fully functional
without them (verified by an automated test that blocks both CDNs).

`scroll-world` is vendored **unmodified** into `assets/js/vendor/scroll-world.js` from the
skill's `references/scrub-engine.js`. It is used in **stills-only mode**: each scene supplies
a `still` and no `clip`, so the engine cross-dissolves photographs with a scroll-driven
camera push instead of scrubbing generated video. That path needs none of the skill's paid
asset pipeline (Monid CLI / Seedance, Higgsfield, GPT Image 2) and costs nothing to run.
Adding a `clip:` to any scene in `assets/js/modules/world.js` upgrades that one scene to
real video scrubbing without touching anything else.

## Banner casting

The banner tells one child's journey, and the casting is load-bearing. Scenes 3–5
(`programs/nursery.webp`, `gallery/g-read.webp`, `gallery/g-learn.webp`) are **the same girl
with braids**, from the same Indian Montessori shoot — working the golden beads, then
writing, then standing at the India map taking the class. Scenes 1–2 are wide group frames
from that same shoot, so the story opens on a school and narrows onto her.

Substituting outside stock into any of these will break the continuity that makes it read as
one child rather than five unrelated photographs. A search of Pexels for alternatives found
that Indian *preschool-age* children essentially only exist in this one shoot: toddler-age
stock is overwhelmingly European, and "graduation" stock is university adults.
